Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 01 January 2026

Vardi Taiyari respects the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide login and account-related features, we may collect basic personal information such as:

  • Email address
  • Login credentials (encrypted)

We do NOT collect sensitive personal information such as Aadhaar number, PAN, bank details, or payment information.

2. Authentication

Users can log in using Gmail or email and password. Login information is used only for authentication and account management purposes.

3. Non-Personal Information

The app may collect non-personal information such as:

  • Device type
  • Android version
  • App usage statistics
  • Crash logs

This data is used only to improve app performance and stability.

4. App Content

All educational content available in the app is manually created and verified by humans. This app does NOT use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to generate educational content.

5. Permissions Used

The app uses the following permissions:

  • Internet – to load content, login, and sync data
  • Media / Storage – to download and display images within the app gallery

No other sensitive permissions such as camera, microphone, contacts, or location are accessed.

6. Image Download & Gallery

Downloaded images are stored locally on the user’s device and are used only for viewing within the app. The app does not upload personal images without user action.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable security measures to protect user data. User information is not sold, rented, or shared with unauthorized third parties.

8. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as Google Play Services or Firebase for authentication, analytics, and performance monitoring. These services follow their own privacy policies.

9. Children’s Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
